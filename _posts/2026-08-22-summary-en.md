Success could pave the way for FDA approval and broader application of this approach to other cancer types, potentially transforming cancer treatment. The trial included 1,137 patients whose melanoma had been surgically removed, and the vaccine was designed to target tumor-specific neoantigens. The companies plan to seek FDA approval based on these results, which were announced in a press release on August 19, 2026.

twitter · ylecun · Aug 21, 05:09

**Background**: Neoantigens are tumor-specific antigens arising from somatic mutations, absent in normal tissues, making them ideal targets for personalized therapies. mRNA vaccines work by instructing cells to produce a protein that triggers an immune response against these neoantigens. This trial is the culmination of years of research into personalized cancer vaccines, which have long been seen as promising but lacked definitive clinical proof.

The discussion can help developers avoid overusing LLMs for tasks better suited to embeddings, such as semantic search and clustering.
