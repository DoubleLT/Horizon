## [ProteinGuide Officially Released for On-the-Fly Protein Design](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2083181975381131378) ⭐️ 7.0/10

ProteinGuide has been officially released, as announced by Jennifer Listgarten on Twitter. The tool enables conditioning pre-trained protein generative models on user-specified properties without retraining, using Bayes' Rule at each sampling step. This release provides a general framework for protein engineering, allowing researchers to guide generative models like ProteinMPNN and ESM3 with experimental data or desired properties. It could significantly accelerate protein design by eliminating the need for costly retraining, impacting fields like drug discovery and enzyme engineering. ProteinGuide is a form of 'classifier guidance' for discrete generative models, unifying masked language, autoregressive, diffusion, and flow-matching models. The preprint is available on arXiv (2505.04823), and the code is on GitHub (junhaobearxiong/proteinguide).

twitter · berkeley_ai · Jul 31, 13:24

**Background**: Protein sequence generative models are transforming protein engineering, but conditioning them on auxiliary information typically requires retraining. ProteinGuide provides an 'on-the-fly' method to condition these models using property predictive models, applying Bayes' Rule at each sampling step. This allows researchers to blend wet-lab data with pretrained generative models for library design without additional training.

## [Can AI Agents Conduct Open-Ended AI Research?](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2083299235911364754) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A tweet by Yann LeCun retweeting Sayash Kapoor questions whether AI agents can conduct open-ended AI research, highlighting that most current evaluations focus on narrow, verifiable tasks. This discussion is grounded in a recent arXiv paper proposing a third evaluation method using shadow evaluations with original authors as reviewers. This question is central to forecasts of explosive AI progress, as the ability of AI agents to automate open-ended research would have transformative impacts on science and technology. The proposed shadow evaluation method could provide more accurate measurements of AI R&D automation progress, influencing research priorities and investment decisions. The arXiv paper (2607.27191) criticizes current evaluations for either being too narrow (excluding open-ended research) or relying on blind peer review, which is overstretched and stochastic. The proposed third method gives agents the central research question from an unpublished paper and has the original authors grade the work, providing open-ended tasks with uncontaminated questions and expert reviewers.

twitter · ylecun · Jul 31, 21:10

**Background**: AI agents are increasingly used in research, but evaluating their ability to conduct open-ended research is challenging. Traditional benchmarks focus on narrow tasks with clear answers, while blind peer review of AI-generated papers is unreliable due to reviewer overload and variability. The shadow evaluation method aims to address these gaps by using domain experts to assess agent performance on real, open-ended problems.
