## [AI Generates Atlas of Over 1 Billion Protein Structures](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2059973453902463040) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A newly released AI tool, ESMFold2, has generated an atlas of over one billion predicted protein structures, as reported by Nature. The model achieves state-of-the-art performance in protein structure prediction without requiring multiple sequence alignments (MSAs). This breakthrough dramatically expands the known protein structure universe, enabling faster drug discovery and deeper understanding of molecular biology. It also democratizes access to high-quality structure prediction with just nine lines of code. ESMFold2 is built on ESMC representations and delivers leading accuracy for protein-protein and antibody-antigen complexes. The -Fast model variant performs particularly well on antibody-antigen complexes, a critical area for therapeutics.

twitter · ylecun · May 28, 12:21

**Background**: Protein structure prediction is a fundamental challenge in biology, as a protein's function is determined by its 3D shape. Traditional methods like AlphaFold rely on MSAs of related sequences, which can be computationally expensive. ESMFold2 uses a language model approach to predict structures directly from single sequences, greatly reducing computational cost.

## [Berkeley AI Releases Recon for Reasoning Synthesis](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2060139177837297889) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Berkeley AI researchers released Recon, a new method for reasoning synthesis in user modeling that uses action reconstruction to score reasoning traces by their predictive power, challenging the common practice of post-hoc rationalization. This matters because Recon provides a more faithful alternative to post-hoc rationalization, which often produces plausible but inaccurate explanations, potentially improving the reliability of user models in AI systems. Recon achieves up to 70% win rate over post-hoc rationalization in user modeling tasks, as reported on the project website. The method is detailed in a recent arXiv paper (arXiv:2605.26969).

twitter · berkeley_ai · May 28, 23:20

**Background**: User modeling aims to infer users' mental states or reasoning from their actions. Post-hoc rationalization, a common approach, generates explanations after the fact, which may not reflect actual reasoning. Recon instead uses action reconstruction to evaluate reasoning traces based on how well they predict future actions.
