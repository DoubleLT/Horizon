## [LeCun Shares Hamiltonian JEPA World Model Paper](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2106727016623931894) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Yann LeCun retweeted a post by Muzaffer Kal highlighting a new paper titled "Hamiltonian JEPA: Action-Conditioned World Models with an Inherited Control State" by Tamim Zoabi and one other author. The paper proposes combining JEPA-style predictive architectures with Hamiltonian dynamics to build action-conditioned world models. This work sits at the intersection of energy-based models, world models, and control theory, which are central to LeCun's long-term vision for AI that learns predictive representations rather than reconstructing raw inputs. If successful, such action-conditioned world models could improve planning and reinforcement learning agents by grounding them in physically consistent dynamics. The paper introduces an "inherited control state" within a Hamiltonian formulation, meaning the model treats control inputs as part of the dynamical system's state rather than as external perturbations. The tweet itself provides minimal context, and the paper is hosted on arXiv with limited community engagement so far.

twitter · ylecun · Oct 4, 12:43

**Background**: JEPA, or Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture, is a self-supervised learning approach championed by Yann LeCun in which a model predicts abstract representations of missing or future data rather than reconstructing raw pixels. Hamiltonian dynamics is a formalism in physics and control theory that describes systems in terms of energy conservation, often used to model stable, reversible motion. Combining the two aims to give learned world models physically grounded, energy-aware structure for action-conditioned prediction.
