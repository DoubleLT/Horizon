## [Shift to Realistic Training for Robot RL Policies](https://twitter.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2079250795422236828) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A tweet from @lukas_m_ziegler highlights a shift in reinforcement learning for robotics: instead of training policies in synthetic, untextured environments, researchers are now using more realistic simulations to improve perceptive policies. This shift addresses a key limitation of sim-to-real transfer, where policies trained in unrealistic simulations often fail in the real world. More realistic training environments could lead to robots that generalize better to complex, real-world tasks. The tweet references FlexionAI's observation that historically, RL policies were trained in synthetic, untextured environments, which limited the development of perceptive policies that rely on visual or other sensory input.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Jul 20, 17:03

**Background**: Sim-to-real transfer is a common approach in robotics where policies are trained in simulation and then deployed on real robots. However, the 'reality gap'—differences between simulation and reality—often causes poor performance. Perceptive policies use sensory data (e.g., vision) to make decisions, making them more sensitive to environmental realism.
