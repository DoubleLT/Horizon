The model scales JEPA predictors to 8B parameters and is trained on a large multi-embodiment dataset, but the tweet provides limited technical details on architecture specifics or evaluation benchmarks. Scaling laws describe how model performance improves predictably as model size, data, and compute increase, a phenomenon well-established in large language models.
