## [AsymFlow: Low-Rank Velocity Improves Pixel Generation](https://twitter.com/unknown/status/2055080480173932789) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A new paper introduces Asymmetric Flow Modeling (AsymFlow), which proposes using a low-rank velocity subspace to improve pixel generation beyond JiT x0-prediction. This approach achieves state-of-the-art results (1.57 FID on ImageNet 256x256) and enables finetuning from pretrained latent flow models to pixel-space generation without retraining from scratch, potentially accelerating progress in generative modeling. AsymFlow restricts noise prediction to a low-rank subspace while keeping data prediction full-dimensional, addressing a limitation of JiT x0-prediction for pixel generation. The paper also demonstrates the first successful bridge from pretrained latent flow models to pixel-space generation through finetuning.

twitter · unknown · May 15, 00:19

**Background**: Flow matching models learn to transform noise into data by predicting a velocity field. JiT (just-in-time) x0-prediction is a common technique where the model directly predicts the clean data point, but it may be insufficient for high-dimensional pixel generation. AsymFlow introduces an asymmetric parameterization that improves efficiency and quality.
