## [LeVJEPA Simplifies Video Self-Supervised Learning, Cuts Compute 5-20x](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2094091928589520934) ⭐️ 8.0/10

LeVJEPA, a new video encoder, achieves state-of-the-art performance in video self-supervised learning with 5-20x less compute than prior methods, while simplifying the training pipeline by removing heuristics like EMA target encoders and stop-gradient. This advance could significantly lower the computational barrier for video representation learning, making it more accessible to researchers and enabling more efficient training on large-scale video data. It also aligns with the trend toward simpler, more principled self-supervised methods in AI. LeVJEPA is the first video encoder trained under LeJEPA's collapse-free objective, using only one encoder, one loss, and one hyperparameter. It dispenses with architectural machinery such as EMA target encoders, stop-gradient, capacity-limited predictors, and pixel-space reconstruction decoders.

twitter · ylecun · Aug 30, 15:56

**Background**: Self-supervised learning (SSL) trains models on unlabeled data by creating pretext tasks, such as predicting masked parts of the input. In video SSL, models learn spatio-temporal representations from videos, which contain rich information about object motion and temporal causality. Previous video SSL methods often relied on complex architectural tricks to prevent representation collapse, where all inputs map to the same output. LeVJEPA simplifies this by using a joint-embedding predictive architecture with a collapse-free objective, achieving efficiency without sacrificing performance.
