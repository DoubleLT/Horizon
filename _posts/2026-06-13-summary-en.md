## [Flow Reversal Steering Guides Diffusion VLAs with High-Level Actions](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2065286923149853038) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers propose Flow Reversal Steering (FRS), a method that enables diffusion-based vision-language-action (VLA) models to be steered by high-level actions, such as those from VLM reasoning, by reversing the flow process to find latent noises and map them to nearby generalist action modes. This approach bridges high-level reasoning from VLMs with low-level robot control, potentially improving the flexibility and generalization of robotic policies without retraining. It could accelerate the deployment of more capable and adaptable robots in real-world tasks. FRS takes suboptimal but reasonable actions, reverses the flow matching process to obtain their latent noises, and then maps them to nearby action modes of a generalist policy. The method is designed for flow matching generalists and does not require additional training.

twitter · berkeley_ai · Jun 12, 04:15

**Background**: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models integrate visual perception, language understanding, and action generation for robotics. Diffusion-based VLAs use iterative denoising to produce actions, but steering them with high-level reasoning has been challenging. Flow Reversal Steering leverages the invertibility of flow matching to guide the action generation process.

## [Challenges in RL Training of Diffusion Policies](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2065023523274535408) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Sergey Levine highlights that training diffusion or flow-based policies with reinforcement learning is notoriously difficult due to instability in backpropagation through time (BPTT) and other issues. This challenge is a key bottleneck in applying diffusion models to robot learning and continuous control, where RL fine-tuning is often needed for task-specific performance. The tweet specifically mentions BPTT instability and difficulties with RL over diffusion trajectories, which are known issues that recent work like Diffusion Policy Policy Optimization (DPPO) aims to address.

twitter · berkeley_ai · Jun 11, 10:49

**Background**: Diffusion policies are a class of generative models that produce actions by iteratively denoising random noise, offering high expressiveness. However, training them with RL requires backpropagating through the denoising steps (BPTT), which can be unstable and computationally expensive. Recent research, such as DPPO, proposes algorithmic frameworks to stabilize this process.
