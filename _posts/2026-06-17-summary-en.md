Researchers introduced Neuro-JEPA, a foundation model for multimodal neuroimaging that uses a sparse latent predictive approach based on the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA). This model could advance AI-driven analysis of brain data, enabling better diagnosis and understanding of neurological conditions by learning robust representations from multimodal neuroimaging. Neuro-JEPA leverages sparse latent representations and predictive masking, similar to Brain-JEPA, which achieved state-of-the-art results in demographic prediction and disease diagnosis. twitter · ylecun · Jun 16, 14:26

**Background**: Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) is a family of models introduced by Yann LeCun that learn by predicting high-level embeddings of unseen parts of input, rather than reconstructing pixels. This approach is inspired by human-like learning and is used in various domains including vision and video. Neuro-JEPA applies this architecture to neuroimaging, aiming to capture brain dynamics and functional connectivity.
