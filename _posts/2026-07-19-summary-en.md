## [LightTact: Tactile Sensor Detects Touch Without Deformation](https://twitter.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2078500834787185051) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have introduced LightTact, a visual-tactile fingertip sensor that detects light contact without requiring physical deformation, using an optics-based principle with ambient-blocking optical configuration. This breakthrough overcomes a fundamental limitation of traditional tactile sensors that rely on deformation, enabling robots to sense light touch, water, cream, or soft films—expanding capabilities in robotics, haptics, and human-robot interaction. LightTach achieves pixel-level contact segmentation robust to material properties, contact forces, surface appearance, and environmental lighting, and provides naturally aligned visual-tactile multimodal signals suitable for robot learning.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Jul 18, 15:23

**Background**: Traditional tactile sensors, such as piezoresistive or capacitive types, measure contact through deformation of a soft surface. This limits their ability to detect light or non-deforming contacts like touching water or soft films. LightTact uses an optical method that makes contact directly visible, bypassing the need for deformation.

## [LingBot-Map: Real-Time 3D Reconstruction from a Single Camera](https://twitter.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2078054148231069793) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Robbyant team open-sourced LingBot-Map, a feed-forward 3D foundation model that reconstructs scenes in real time from a single ordinary camera at nearly 20 FPS. This breakthrough enables live 3D mapping from a single camera, which is crucial for robotics, AR/VR, and autonomous navigation, and the open-source release accelerates research and practical applications. LingBot-Map uses a Geometric Context Transformer that unifies coordinate grounding, dense geometric cues, and long-range drift correction within a streaming framework, recovering camera pose and scene structure from video streams.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Jul 17, 09:48

**Background**: Traditional 3D reconstruction from a single camera often requires offline processing or multiple viewpoints, limiting real-time applications. LingBot-Map is a feed-forward model that processes streaming video frames sequentially, updating the map as the camera moves, achieving real-time performance without post-processing.

The design is based on a kinematic concept from Laval University. This demonstration highlights an elegant mechanical design that achieves high precision and stability with simplicity, potentially inspiring low-cost, high-performance XYZ platforms for applications like 3D printing or pick-and-place robotics. The tripteron is a 3-DOF linear manipulator where all three input actuators are oriented in the same direction, allowing for nearly unlimited travel along that axis. **Background**: Parallel manipulators use multiple limbs to connect a moving platform to a base, offering high stiffness and precision. The tripteron is a type of Cartesian parallel manipulator, where the limbs are aligned with the Cartesian axes, simplifying control and mapping of actuator positions to end-effector coordinates.
