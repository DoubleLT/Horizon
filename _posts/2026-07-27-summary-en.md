Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated in an Axios interview that distillation—learning from other AI models—is fundamental to intelligence and should be allowed, even for open-source models distilling from closed ones. This statement from a leading industry figure could influence the ongoing debate about the legality and ethics of model distillation, especially regarding open-source AI development and competition with closed-source models. Huang was responding to a question about whether open-source model companies should be allowed to distill closed models, and he argued that learning from others is a natural part of intelligence.

twitter · ylecun · Jul 25, 08:31

**Background**: Knowledge distillation is a technique where a smaller, cheaper model is trained to mimic the outputs of a larger, more capable model. This allows the smaller model to achieve similar performance on specific tasks at a fraction of the cost. The practice has become controversial as some closed-model developers argue it infringes on their intellectual property.
