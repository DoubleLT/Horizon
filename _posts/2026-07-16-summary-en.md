## [Anthropic: Robot Control Interface Matters More Than LLM](https://twitter.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2077078932495704166) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Anthropic published a robotics study showing that the same LLM performs drastically differently depending on the control interface, with raw torque control mostly failing while supervising a pretrained policy succeeds. This finding challenges the assumption that the model alone determines performance, highlighting that interface design is critical for deploying LLMs in robotics. It could shift research focus toward better control interfaces rather than just scaling models. The study used the same LLM across different control interfaces, finding that raw torque control (directly commanding motor torques) mostly fails, while supervising a pretrained policy (e.g., behavior cloning) yields success. The specific LLM and tasks were not detailed in the tweet.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Jul 14, 17:13

**Background**: In robotics, control interfaces translate high-level commands (e.g., from an LLM) into low-level motor actions. Raw torque control directly sets motor torques, which is challenging because it requires precise modeling of dynamics. Supervised pretrained policies, such as behavior cloning, learn from demonstration data and are more robust. This research suggests that the choice of interface can be more important than the model itself.
