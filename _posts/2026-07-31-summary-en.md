## [Google DeepMind Unveils Gemini Robotics 2 for Whole-Body Humanoid Control](https://twitter.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2082860880719499711) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2, a suite of three models that enable whole-body reasoning for humanoid robots. This includes a vision-language-action (VLA) model that converts vision and language input into motor control, controlling the entire robot from feet to fingertips. This marks a significant advancement in physical AI, moving beyond upper-body control to whole-body dynamics, which is crucial for humanoid robots to perform complex, real-world tasks. It could accelerate the deployment of humanoid robots in industries like manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare, and sets a new benchmark for embodied AI. The suite includes three models: Gemini Robotics 2 (the VLA), an embodied reasoning VLM (Gemini Robotics ER 2), and an on-device VLA. The VLA and on-device models remain gated, while Gemini Robotics ER 2 is in public preview. Demonstrations show the software controlling the updated Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoid.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Jul 30, 16:08

**Background**: Vision-language-action (VLA) models are a class of AI models that combine visual perception, language understanding, and motor control, enabling robots to interpret instructions and execute actions. Previous models often controlled only the upper body, limiting tasks to table-top operations. Whole-body control requires coordinating all joints and limbs, which is more complex but essential for tasks like walking, climbing, or manipulating large objects. Gemini Robotics 2 aims to unify these capabilities under a single model.
