## [Music-JEPA: Learning a World Model of Piano Sound](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2081758662159200422) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers propose Music-JEPA, a world model for piano sound learned via the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA), extending self-supervised learning from images to audio. This work applies JEPA, a promising self-supervised learning method, to a new modality (audio), potentially enabling more efficient and robust representation learning for music and sound without labeled data. Music-JEPA learns a world model that predicts the evolution of piano sound given actions, similar to how I-JEPA predicts image representations from context blocks. The model is non-generative, focusing on representation prediction rather than pixel-level generation.

twitter · ylecun · Jul 27, 15:08

**Background**: JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture) is a self-supervised learning method introduced by Yann LeCun and colleagues, originally for images. It learns representations by predicting the embedding of a target block from a context block in the same image, without requiring explicit labels. A world model in AI is a neural network that builds an internal representation of an environment and predicts how it changes over time in response to actions.
