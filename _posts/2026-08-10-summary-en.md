The lecture, delivered on December 8, 2025, covers the role of creative destruction in economic growth. This lecture is significant because it presents the foundational ideas behind the 2025 Nobel Prize in Economics, which recognized Aghion and Peter Howitt for their work on creative destruction and growth. It provides valuable insights for economists, policymakers, and anyone interested in how innovation drives economic progress. twitter · ylecun · Aug 9, 21:44

Aghion and Howitt formalized this idea into a mathematical model showing how innovation drives sustained growth through a cycle of new products replacing old ones. This work has been influential in the field of endogenous growth theory.
