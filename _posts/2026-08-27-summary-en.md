## [LpWM: Sparse Representations for World Models](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2092698202101170267) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Yann LeCun retweeted an announcement introducing LpWM (Latent Particle World Model), a new approach that argues dense Gaussian representations are not mandatory for world models, advocating for sparse representations instead. This challenges a common assumption in model-based reinforcement learning and could lead to more efficient and scalable world models, impacting AI research and applications in decision-making and video prediction. LpWM autonomously discovers keypoints, bounding boxes, and object masks from video data, and is scaled to real-world multi-object datasets. The paper is an ICLR 2026 Oral and is available on arXiv (2603.04553).

twitter · ylecun · Aug 26, 19:38

**Background**: World models are internal representations that AI systems use to predict future states of their environment. Traditional approaches often use dense Gaussian distributions to represent uncertainty, but sparse representations, which use only a few active components, can be more efficient and interpretable. LpWM builds on object-centric learning, where the model decomposes scenes into discrete objects, and uses latent particles to represent these objects sparsely.
