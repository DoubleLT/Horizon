## [MemFail Framework Accepted to EMNLP 2026](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2091757742356881808) ⭐️ 6.0/10

MemFail could provide a standardized way to evaluate and improve these systems, benefiting both academia and industry. **Background**: Large language models (LLMs) often use external memory systems to store and retrieve information across conversations, but these systems can fail in various ways. MemFail aims to stress-test these failure modes by providing a diagnostic benchmark that isolates specific operations like summarization, storage, and retrieval. EMNLP is a top conference for natural language processing, and acceptance there signals significant contribution to the field.
