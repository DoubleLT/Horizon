The model uses a JEPA-style pretraining framework to learn action-relevant state transitions from video data. This advancement reduces the need for costly human-annotated action labels, enabling robots to learn from vast amounts of unlabeled video data. It could accelerate the development of more general and capable robotic systems. VLA-JEPA sidesteps common pitfalls in latent-action objectives by focusing on action-relevant state transitions rather than pixel variation. The model is integrated into LeRobot, a PyTorch-based framework for real-world robotics that provides pretrained models and datasets.

twitter · ylecun · Jun 8, 21:50

**Background**: Traditional vision-language-action (VLA) models often require explicit action supervision, which is expensive to obtain. JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) is a self-supervised learning approach that learns representations by predicting latent representations of future observations. LeRobot is an open-source framework by Hugging Face that aims to democratize robotics AI by providing tools, models, and datasets.
