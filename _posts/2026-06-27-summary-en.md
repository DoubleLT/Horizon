Photoroom has released PRX Pixel, an open-source 7-billion-parameter text-to-image model that generates images directly in pixel space, bypassing the need for a VAE. This approach simplifies the generation pipeline and could lead to higher fidelity images, challenging the dominant latent diffusion paradigm. It provides the community with a novel, open-source alternative for text-to-image generation. PRX Pixel uses a Qwen3-VL text encoder instead of T5 or Gemma, and feeds the generation resolution into the timestep modulation. The model is available on Hugging Face with a 1024x1024 pipeline.

twitter · ylecun · Jun 26, 20:21

**Background**: Most modern text-to-image models, like Stable Diffusion, operate in a compressed latent space using a VAE to reduce computational cost. Pixel-space models directly denoise raw RGB pixels, which can be more computationally intensive but may preserve finer details. PRX Pixel is a pixel-space variant of Photoroom's earlier PRX model.

Sergey Levine shared a method to learn a model that provides shaped 'process rewards' for robotic reinforcement learning, which automatically evolves as the policy improves. This approach could significantly improve sample efficiency and training stability in robotic RL by providing denser, adaptive reward signals, potentially accelerating progress in real-world robot learning. The process rewards are shaped and evolve automatically as the policy gets better, enabling step-level credit assignment without manual reward engineering.

twitter · berkeley_ai · Jun 26, 04:17

**Background**: In reinforcement learning, rewards guide the agent's learning. Traditional sparse rewards provide feedback only at goal achievement, while shaped rewards offer intermediate feedback.
