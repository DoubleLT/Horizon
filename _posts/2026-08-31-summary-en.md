## [LeVJEPA Simplifies Video Self-Supervised Learning with 5-20x Less Compute](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2094091928589520934) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Yann LeCun retweeted a post announcing LeVJEPA, a new method that simplifies video self-supervised learning by eliminating architectural heuristics like EMA target encoders and stop-gradients, achieving comparable or better performance with 5-20x less compute. This breakthrough could significantly reduce the computational cost of video pretraining, making it more accessible to researchers and smaller organizations. It also advances the field of self-supervised learning by demonstrating that complex heuristics may be unnecessary, potentially influencing future model designs. LeVJEPA is the first video encoder trained under LeJEPA's collapse-free objective, using only one encoder, one loss, and one hyperparameter. It dispenses with EMA target encoders, stop-gradients, capacity-limited predictors (as in V-JEPA), and pixel-space reconstruction decoders (as in VideoMAE).

twitter · ylecun · Aug 30, 15:56

**Background**: Self-supervised learning (SSL) for videos aims to learn representations from unlabeled video data, which contains rich temporal and causal information. Traditional SSL methods often rely on complex architectural tricks to prevent representation collapse, such as contrastive learning, generative reconstruction, or cross-modal agreement. LeVJEPA builds on the JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) framework, which avoids collapse by predicting in latent space rather than pixel space.
