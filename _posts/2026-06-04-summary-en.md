This work addresses the critical problem of model collapse in diffusion models, which can degrade performance when models are trained on synthetic data. It offers a potential post-training fix without requiring retraining, which is significant for maintaining model quality in iterative training pipelines. This approach differs from traditional prevention methods that modify the training process.

**Background**: Diffusion models generate data by gradually denoising random noise. Mode collapse occurs when the model fails to produce diverse outputs, often due to training on synthetic data or other factors. Noise optimization techniques have been explored to improve generation quality by selecting or optimizing the initial noise at inference time.
