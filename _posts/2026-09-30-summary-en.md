## [SpaceX's Starship Attempts First Orbital Flight](https://twitter.com/SpaceX/status/2104654998034690329) ⭐️ 9.0/10

SpaceX announced the liftoff of Starship's first orbital flight attempt, marking the first time the fully integrated two-stage vehicle flew as a complete system. The launch took place from Starbase in Texas on April 20, 2023, and the vehicle exploded roughly four minutes after liftoff. This was a historic step toward the first fully reusable orbital rocket, which could dramatically lower launch costs and reshape the aerospace industry. Its success or failure also directly affects NASA's Artemis program, since a crewed Starship variant is contracted to land astronauts on the Moon. Starship consists of the Super Heavy booster and the Starship spacecraft, both powered by Raptor engines burning liquid methane and liquid oxygen. The first integrated flight ended in an explosion about four minutes after liftoff, and the program has since suffered further setbacks, including the failure of the first four Block 2 upper stages in 2025.

twitter · SpaceX · Sep 28, 19:30

**Background**: Starship is a two-stage, fully reusable super heavy-lift launch vehicle under development by SpaceX, intended as the successor to Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy. Both stages are designed to return and land vertically for reuse, and the upper stage can operate as a standalone spacecraft carrying crew or cargo. SpaceX began concept work on a super heavy-lift reusable vehicle as early as 2005, introduced the current design and name in 2018, and has followed an iterative, prototype-heavy test approach.
