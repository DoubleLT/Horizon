## [WARP-RM Improves Reward Modeling via Time-Sampling Modulation](https://twitter.com/berkeley_ai/status/2074548080050086115) ⭐️ 7.0/10

WARP-RM (Warp-Augmented Relative Progress Reward Modeling) is a self-supervised model that learns a dense, signed, relative progress signal from raw demonstrations by training on temporally-warped playback augmentation. It modulates time-sampling on a few high-quality human demonstrations to improve reward model training. This approach addresses the challenge of mixed-quality demonstrations in imitation learning, enabling more efficient robot learning from human data. In T-shirt folding, WARP-RM increased throughput from 31.6 to 56.3 shirts per hour, a 78% improvement, demonstrating significant practical impact. WARP-RM is fully self-supervised and does not require costly human annotations for subtask boundaries. It uses the learned reward estimates to filter and re-weight action chunks during behavior cloning, improving policy performance.

twitter · berkeley_ai · Jul 7, 17:36

**Background**: Reward modeling is crucial in reinforcement learning for providing feedback to agents. Traditional methods often rely on absolute temporal progress proxies, which suffer from label noise, or require human annotations. WARP-RM introduces a novel self-supervised approach that learns relative progress from raw demonstrations without such limitations.
