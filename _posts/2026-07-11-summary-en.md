Chelsea Finn announced she will give a talk at the ICML RLxF workshop on moving beyond the scalar reward bottleneck for both robotics and large language models. This talk addresses a fundamental limitation in reinforcement learning—the scalar reward bottleneck—which hinders the development of more capable and general AI systems. Insights from this talk could influence future reward design in both robotics and LLM alignment. The scalar reward bottleneck refers to the limitations of using a single number to capture all aspects of desired behavior. Researchers have proposed alternatives such as multi-objective reward models and shaping landscapes to provide richer feedback.
