The design, detailed in a December 2024 paper, uses simple cable actuation for versatile grasping across scales. This work offers a universal geometric design principle for soft robots, potentially simplifying fabrication and control while enabling lifelike movements. The logarithmic spiral shape provides large curvature changes along the body, giving higher flexibility near the tip without needing multiple independently actuated segments. The robots are fabricated by discretizing and uncurling the spiral, allowing easy scaling from centimeters to meters.

twitter · lukas_m_ziegler · Aug 2, 11:46

**Background**: Soft robotics aims to create machines from compliant materials that can safely interact with delicate objects and environments. Many natural appendages, such as octopus arms and elephant trunks, exhibit a tightly packed logarithmic spiral shape, which allows efficient curling and uncurling for grasping. Traditional soft manipulators often rely on multiple actuated segments to achieve flexibility, but SpiRobs achieve this through a single continuous spiral geometry.
