## [Photoroom Releases PRX Pixel: Open-Source 7B Pixel-Space T2I Model](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2070603360823071199) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Photoroom has released PRX Pixel, an open-source 7B-parameter text-to-image diffusion model that generates images directly in pixel space, eliminating the need for a VAE. The model is available on Hugging Face and GitHub under a permissive license. PRX Pixel represents a shift from latent diffusion to pixel-space generation, potentially reducing artifacts and improving image fidelity. As an open-source model, it democratizes access to advanced text-to-image technology and may spur innovation in the community. The model uses a Qwen3-VL text encoder instead of T5/Gemma, and feeds generation resolution into timestep modulation. It was pretrained on hundreds of millions of images, followed by supervised fine-tuning and preference alignment.

twitter · ylecun · Jun 26, 20:21

**Background**: Most text-to-image models, like Stable Diffusion, operate in latent space using a VAE to compress images into a lower-dimensional representation before denoising. Pixel-space models directly process raw RGB pixels, which can avoid compression artifacts but historically required more compute. PRX Pixel builds on the PRX transformer-based diffusion framework and is related to recent work like PixelDiT.

## [Flow Reversal Steering for Robotic Behavior Translation](https://twitter.com/StanfordAILab/status/2070736949254705303) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Stanford AI Lab shares a research idea on flow reversal steering, a method that translates a rough sense of what to do and a VLA prior into successful robot behavior. This approach could enable robots to leverage generalist policies and prior knowledge to perform tasks more effectively, advancing the field of robotic manipulation and generalization. Flow reversal steering works by taking suboptimal but reasonable actions, finding their latent noises by passing them through the flow policy in reverse, and mapping them to nearby generalist action modes.

twitter · StanfordAILab · Jun 27, 05:12

**Background**: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are large-scale pretrained models that serve as foundations for generalist robot manipulation. Flow matching generalists use flow-based generative models to produce robot actions. Flow Reversal Steering (FRS) is a method that improves the quality of actions from such generalists by steering suboptimal actions toward better modes.
