## [Single Image to 3D World in Minutes with Image-Blaster](https://twitter.com/drfeifei/status/tweet-2055022392569905411) ⭐️ 8.0/10

World Labs team member Neilson Koerner-Safrata released image-blaster, an open-source 3D generation toolkit for Claude Code that transforms a single image into a fully explorable 3D environment with meshes, physics, lighting, and audio in minutes. This tool significantly lowers the barrier for 3D content creation, enabling rapid prototyping and immersive experiences from a single photo, which could impact gaming, VR, and digital twin industries. Image-blaster is built as a skillset for Claude Code, leveraging AI to generate a fully meshed 3D world with physics and audio. It is open-source and available on GitHub, with a demo on World Labs' community showcase.

twitter · Fei-Fei Li · May 14, 20:28

**Background**: Single-image 3D reconstruction is a long-standing challenge in computer vision. Recent advances like TripoSR and LRM can generate 3D meshes from a single image in seconds, but image-blaster goes further by creating an entire explorable environment with interactive elements.
