## [World Labs Unveils image-blaster for 3D from Single Image](https://twitter.com/drfeifei/status/tweet-2055022392569905411) ⭐️ 8.0/10

World Labs, co-founded by Fei-Fei Li, announced image-blaster, an open-source tool that converts a single image into a fully meshed 3D world with physics, lighting, and audio in minutes. This tool significantly lowers the barrier for 3D content creation, enabling rapid prototyping for game developers, VR/AR creators, and designers without requiring specialized 3D modeling skills. image-blaster is built as a skillset for Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, and is open-sourced on GitHub under the repository neilsonnn/image-blaster.

twitter · Fei-Fei Li · May 14, 20:28

**Background**: Single-image 3D reconstruction is a challenging computer vision problem. Traditional methods often require multiple views or lengthy optimization. Recent advances like Unique3D and InstantMesh have improved efficiency, but image-blaster integrates generation with a full environment including physics and audio.
