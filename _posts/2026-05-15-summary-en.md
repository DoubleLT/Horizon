## [World Labs' image-blaster: Single Image to 3D World](https://twitter.com/drfeifei/status/tweet-2055022392569905411) ⭐️ 8.0/10

World Labs, co-founded by Fei-Fei Li, introduced image-blaster, a tool that converts a single image into a fully meshed 3D world in minutes. This breakthrough significantly accelerates 3D content creation, enabling rapid prototyping for gaming, VR, and simulation, and democratizing 3D modeling for non-experts. Image-blaster uses World Labs' Marble model to generate explorable environments and includes a nano-banana model for source cleanup and object reference images.

twitter · Fei-Fei Li · May 14, 20:28

**Background**: Traditional 3D reconstruction from a single image is challenging due to missing depth information. Recent AI models like Unique3D and InstantMesh have improved efficiency, but World Labs' approach integrates multiple generation models for a complete pipeline.
